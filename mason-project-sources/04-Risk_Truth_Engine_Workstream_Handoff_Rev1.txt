Methodology agreed:
Risk Truth is a control/reconciliation layer, not a second direct-cost estimate. Every risk records its existing tender baseline and cost location before residual exposure is considered. Quantifiable physical consequences return to Resource Build-Up/QS. Specialist-engine exposures remain in their specialist pathways. CA01/CA02 contain only distinct residual cross-cutting risk and are not generic percentages.

Key decisions made:
- CA01 = Design / Scope Residual Risk.
- CA02 = Construction / Execution Residual Risk.
- Likelihood/Impact does not automatically create dollars.
- Positive residual additions require a distinct basis, integration target and duplication check.
- One cost may have several contributing risks but must be costed once.
- Weather remains Weather Risk -> CA10.
- RED duplication or programme reconciliation blocks final release.
- Historic tender assumptions and actual outcomes are retained separately.
