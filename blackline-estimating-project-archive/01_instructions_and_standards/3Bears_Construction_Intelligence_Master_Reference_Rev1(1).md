**Purpose:** Make the 3Bears Construction Intelligence Engine persistently available across estimating chats, projects and future workflows without duplicating or fragmenting the source of truth.

## Core principle

The 3Bears Construction Intelligence Engine is a permanent backend evidence system for construction estimating and commercial decision support across New Zealand and Australia.

It is not a second estimate, not a replacement for project drawings/specifications, not a substitute for current supplier quotations, and not a mechanism for silently changing live estimates.

## Mandatory use rule

Before researching or inserting any external rate, productivity, methodology, supplier, standards, escalation, disposal, cartage, market or programme input into an estimate:

1. Search the live Construction Intelligence Register first.
2. Check whether a current relevant record exists.
3. Confirm geography, scope, specification, unit, tax basis, delivery/hire basis, crew/plant conditions, productivity context and validity.
4. Reuse suitable evidence with its evidence status and source references.
5. If evidence is missing, stale or not comparable, research externally.
6. Feed useful new evidence back into the central register under change control.
7. Never silently overwrite a live project estimate from the central register.

## Verification

GREEN requires:

1. **Primary/current evidence**
2. **Independent check**
3. **First-principles/scope check**

AMBER means usable with visible limitations or incomplete verification.  
RED means missing, contradictory, non-comparable or unsuitable for firm use.

A source may be GREEN while its project application remains AMBER or RED.

## Rate intelligence rule

Normalise rates for date, country/region, currency, GST/tax, specification/grade/size, wet/dry hire, operator, fuel, freight, mobilisation, minimums, hire duration, damage waiver, quantity breaks, trade vs retail, supply-only vs installed, testing/certification, inclusions/exclusions and validity.

Search snippets, indices, forums, unsupported AI summaries and tender totals without aligned scope are not direct rates.

## Productivity rule

A task production rate must never be a naked number. Capture where available:

- output and unit
- period / cycle
- crew composition
- plant
- paid vs productive hours
- net task time vs gross elapsed production
- depth / size / geometry / complexity
- access / staging
- material handling
- ground/site conditions
- weather
- testing / hold points
- rework / quality assumptions
- work-front arrangement
- source context and limitations

Do not average materially different complexity classes into one convenient rate.
