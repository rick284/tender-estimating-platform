# Estimating project lessons — preliminary extraction

These are *candidate lessons* based on partial conversation summaries. They do not amend the controlling standards.

- **Release gates:** A chat conversation can continue indefinitely; formal completion and baseline control prevent later discussion from silently changing an approved estimate.
- **Commercial defensibility:** Estimate scope and cost drivers must remain visible so reductions to a tender can be tied to scope, productivity, procurement, risk or margin decisions rather than an unsupported target total.
- **Independent challenge:** Independent verification should uncover omissions, quantities and programme mismatches rather than average reviewer judgments.
- **Source traceability:** Supplier catalogues, rates and benchmark sources need date, currency, GST basis and source mapping to avoid false comparability.
- **Privacy:** Outbound emails and shared files require conscious release controls, not just technical ability to send.

**Status:** Awaiting checks against full originals; not an instruction or approval source.
